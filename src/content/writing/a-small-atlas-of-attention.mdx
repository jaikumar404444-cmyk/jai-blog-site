An atlas is less a picture of a territory than a method for returning to it. The pages in this sequence are deliberately small: they mark a rhythm of encounter, note, and return.

<PullQuote attribution="Demo visual essay">
Attention is a route we make by revisiting what first seemed ordinary.
</PullQuote>

The first image proposes a field. The second starts to number its edges. The third lets the border remain unresolved.

The point is not to extract a single message from the images. It is to let sequence make an argument that a paragraph alone would not.
